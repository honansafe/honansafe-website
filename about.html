About Hong'an Fire Fighting Equipment

We are a direct factory manufacturer of fire protection equipment — building complete fire safety supply for contractors and distributors worldwide.

Who we are

Factory-direct fire protection supply

Hong'an Fire Fighting Equipment Co., Ltd. manufactures and exports fire protection equipment for commercial, industrial and municipal projects. Our product range covers portable fire extinguishers, fire hose and nozzles, hydrant systems, sprinkler systems and fire alarm equipment — allowing engineering contractors to source a complete fire protection package from a single supplier.

We serve buyers in Southeast Asia, the Middle East and South America with OEM/ODM customisation, competitive factory pricing, and export documentation support. Our team communicates directly in English and provides fast, clear responses on pricing, specifications and lead times.
