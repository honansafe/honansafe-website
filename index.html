Product lines

Complete Fire Protection Equipment Range

Six integrated product lines cover the full scope of a building fire protection project — from portable extinguishers to automatic suppression systems.

EXT

Fire Extinguishers & Cabinets

Portable dry powder, water-based and CO₂ extinguishers with wall cabinets and pressure gauges — available in private-label OEM configuration.

Dry powder · Water · CO₂OEM ✓
HOS

Hose, Nozzles & Couplings

Fire hose, hose nozzles, couplings and branch fittings in standard national thread types for export markets.

Hydrant Systems

Indoor & outdoor fire hydrants, hydrant boxes, valves and pump connection equipment for municipal and building networks.

Indoor · Outdoor · ValveProject ✓
SPR

Sprinkler & Water Systems

Sprinkler heads, alarm valves and wet/dry system components for automatic fire suppression installations.

Head · Valve · SystemProject ✓

Detector · Panel · Call pointOEM ✓
