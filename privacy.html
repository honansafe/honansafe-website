Privacy Policy

How we handle the information you share with us.

1. Information we collect

When you contact us through this website, email or WhatsApp, we receive the information you choose to provide — typically your name, company, email address, country, and the details of your inquiry (product interest, quantity, specifications).

2. How we use your information

  • To respond to your inquiries and provide quotations.
  • To communicate about your orders, samples and shipments.
  • To improve our products and services based on market feedback.

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information to third parties.

3. Data retention

We retain inquiry and order communications as long as needed for business purposes, including order fulfilment, warranty support and legal record-keeping requirements.

4. Cookies and website analytics

This website is a static site and does not set advertising cookies. If analytics tools are added in the future, this policy will be updated accordingly.

5. Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time by contacting us at zwanl@hongan119.com.
