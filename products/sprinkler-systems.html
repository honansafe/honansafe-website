Sprinkler & Automatic Water Systems

Components for automatic sprinkler suppression — heads, alarm valves, flow indication and system accessories.

Product line 04

Automatic suppression for commercial & industrial buildings

Sprinkler systems detect and control fire automatically, protecting people and assets before manual firefighting begins. We supply the key components of wet-pipe systems — sprinkler heads, wet alarm valves, flow indicators and associated water system fittings — sized per your hydraulic design.
