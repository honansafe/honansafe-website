Fire Extinguishers & Cabinets

Portable extinguishers for Class A / B / C fire risks — dry powder, water-based and CO₂ — with matching wall cabinets and accessories.

Portable protection for every floor

A complete extinguisher range for commercial, industrial and residential projects. ABC dry powder is the most widely specified type for general buildings; water-based units suit Class A risks; CO₂ extinguishers protect electrical equipment and server rooms.
