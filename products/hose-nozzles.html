Fire Hose, Nozzles & Couplings

Water delivery equipment for hydrant systems — hose, branch nozzles, couplings and fittings in common export configurations.

Product line 02

Reliable water delivery for hydrant networks

Fire hose and branch fittings connect hydrants to the point of firefighting. We supply jacketed fire hose, straight-stream and adjustable nozzles, couplings and accessories sized to match your hydrant network — with thread types confirmed per destination market to avoid installation mismatch.
